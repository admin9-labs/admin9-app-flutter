# Historical Records

The files listed below preserve Foundation-era plans, decisions, acceptance
reports, audit results, and device evidence as recorded at their original phase,
date, source, and artifact. Their original names, commands, hashes, and findings
are historical facts. They are not current Admin9 App Starter specifications,
do not certify current behavior, and do not govern independent forks.

Current technical and contribution guidance is defined by the root README,
current architecture, current Design System modules, contribution guide,
validation guide, and CI. Licensing and trademark terms remain defined by
`LICENSE` and `TRADEMARKS.md`.

Historical collections:

- `docs/audit/admin9-*.md` except current `VALIDATION.md`;
- `docs/architecture/admin9-ui-implementation-plan.md`;
- `docs/architecture/admin9-ui-phase-*-acceptance-report.md`;
- `docs/architecture/evidence/**`;
- `docs/design-system/admin9-design-system-v1-acceptance-report.md`;
- Design System evidence snapshots under `docs/design-system/evidence/**`,
  except the current rule register used by upstream validation;
- `docs/design/admin9-reference-page-briefs.md`;
- `docs/design/phase-0c/**`; and
- `docs/product/admin9-app-experience-baseline.md`.

The `1.0.0` through `1.0.3` sections of
`docs/design-system/CHANGELOG.md` are also historical release records. The
current uncommitted/unreleased repositioning is recorded only under
`Unreleased`; existing Git tags are not moved, recreated, or rewritten.
